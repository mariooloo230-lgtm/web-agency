What actually happens up your flue

Not a quick brush and a wipe of the hands. Here's the process, scroll to follow it through.

01 — BEFORE

Every fire you light leaves soot and tar clinging to the flue walls. Left alone, it builds up for years.

02 — THE SWEEP

Laura works the rods from the fireplace up through the flue, loosening it from the bottom to the top.

03 — AFTER

It's swept down, bagged up, and gone. What's left is a flue that's actually safe to light a fire in again.
