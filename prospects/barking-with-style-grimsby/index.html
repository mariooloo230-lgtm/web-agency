Most salons aren't built for a dog who's already had a bad day.

Multiple dryers going, other dogs barking from crates, a queue of strangers walking past the table. For a calm, confident dog that's background noise. For a nervous one, it can undo the whole appointment before the brush even comes out.

Becky built Barking with Style around the opposite idea: one dog booked in at a time, the full hour, in a quiet room, going at whatever pace that particular dog needs. It's slower to run than a multi-table salon. It's also why dogs who've struggled everywhere else tend to settle here.
