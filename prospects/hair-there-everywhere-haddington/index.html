Why she stopped waiting for people to come to her

After years working from behind a salon chair, Michele found the better fit was bringing the whole thing to people who struggled to get to her instead, whether that's a mobility issue, a rural East Lothian address with no easy way in, or simply no spare afternoon to give up to travel and waiting.

Thirteen years later it's still door to door, and it costs less than booking the same appointment in a shop. Twenty years of hairdressing altogether means the cut and colour hold up to the same standard, it's just the address that's different.
